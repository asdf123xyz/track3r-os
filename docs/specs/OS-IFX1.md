# OS-IFX1: Glide, Overdrive and Bit depth act on the sampler voices

Level C (the sampler voice render is an audio real-time path) · parity PAR-018 (Overdrive), PAR-051 (Bit depth), the Glide of the instrument and of step FX 19 · **no persistent format change**: the instrument fields (glide u16, overdrive u8, bit depth u8) and the FX ids and ranges were stored already; `fx_sanitize` still clamps at the point of use, never in the file.
Tags: **V** verified parity (evidence cited), **N** new Community OS behaviour (documented, one function to reverse), **Q** open question. No claim in this file is V: no recorded observation of the official firmware is in this repository (a PAR number is the label of a row in the maintainer's parity ledger, which is not here, and is not evidence), so the knobs below are this OS's behaviour, pinned by the tests, and their parity is an open research question.

## What each knob does
- **GLIDE (instrument 0..15000 ms; step FX 19, 0..100).** **Q** (parity not recorded): a note whose track played another note before starts at that note's pitch and goes to its own in the glide time; the time is ms x 44.1 samples, in whole samples; a repeat of the same note does not glide; the FX replaces the instrument's time while the track holds it, value 0 is no glide at all; the FX value 0..100 maps onto a time in two linear segments: value <= 50 gives value x 20 ms, above it 1000 + (value - 50) x 280 ms. **N:** how the original firmware ramps the playback rate is an open research question; here the pitch goes through the Slide FX's ramp, linear in semitones (up to one semitone different mid-glide for an octave, none at the ends; it lands exactly). A Slide FX held by the track has the ramp (Slide wins). The time is read when a note starts.
- **OVERDRIVE (instrument 0..100; step FX 38, 0..100).** **N, no parity claim: the official firmware's curve is an open research question** (PAR-018). A parabolic soft clipper on the voice's block after its filter: `u = x g / 2` held to 16 bits, `y = u (2 - |u|)`, i.e. `x g - x g |x g| / 4`, exactly full scale at the clip; the drive `g = 1 + 15 (value / 100)^2`, 1 to 16 (+24 dB). Odd, monotonic, one multiply for the gain and one for the shape. 0 is OFF (the pass is skipped).
- **BIT DEPTH (instrument 4..16; step FX 39, 1..16).** **Q** (parity not recorded): the low `16 - bits` bits of every sample are cleared, a mask, a floor (a mean offset of half a step below zero); 16 is OFF. The FX is 1..16, so it reaches 1 bit, and the instrument is 4..16; how the official firmware limits the two is an open research question. The Bits12 / Bits8 engines of OS-005h are a cap (the lower of the two applies) and keep their rounding when the engine is the reduction in force (`Voice::crush_round`); when the Bit Depth parameter / FX is lower, the mask applies.

## Where it acts
The step FX are forwarded as ModSet / ModEnd of their own groups (seq `FxGroup::Glide` 4, `Overdrive` 19, `BitDepth` 20, `audio/events.h`), held per track by `VoicePool::fx_event` (flash, per event); the transport start ends them. Overdrive and bit depth act on the voice sounding now and on later notes; a live edit of the instrument's OVERDRIVE or BIT DEPTH reaches the sounding voices at the next block (the descriptor is rebuilt: `sample_desc_reads`); the glide is read when a note starts. Sampler voices only (the Modern engine: sample, loop modes, wavetable, granular); the Amiga voices, an SD module and a MIDI instrument have no such input. FX 38 and 39 enter the FX picker (appended: 43 entries).

## Real time
Zero cost when OFF: a voice with overdrive 0 and 16 bits pays one test of two bytes a span in `VoicePool::render` (and the Bits12 / Bits8 block that was there is gone: SRAM_L hot code shrank). ON, one call to `shape_span` (flash, `O3OS_AUDIO_COLD`: called once a span for a voice that uses either, which stretches the rule that COLD is per event, on purpose, to keep SRAM_L's last bytes) runs a tight loop of 8 (depth), 17 (overdrive) or 20 (both) cycles a sample [est from the disassembly], one voice's block 64 samples. No double, no libm, no allocation, bounded by the span.
`diag bench voice | voice_od | voice_bit | voice_odbit` on the unit measures the pass (stop the transport first; `voice` is the OFF reference).
