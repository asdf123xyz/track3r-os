# OS-COREFX — the tracker FX the Modern engine lacks (import fidelity for MOD, S3M, XM, IT)

Status: specified 2026-10-14 (maintainer: go ahead with OS-COREFX after OS-PFX); **CF1 built** (section 4), **CF2 built** (section 5) and **CF3 built** (section 6) · After OS-PFX (Chord 14, Arp 21, Tune 40, Slide 41/42) · **Level C** (sequencer per-tick FX and voice hooks on the real-time path) · **New Community OS behaviour**: core FX in the reserved 43–127 range (ADR 0004 amendment 1); OG FX 0–42 keep their OG ids and ranges (the Panning FX 31, which the OS did not act on before CF2, now acts as this OS's model of it, section 5), and the importers' own pan is the new core FX 57.

## 0. What exists (verified 2026-10-14)

- Steps carry their two FX bytes verbatim; a type outside the table is inert, kept, never written (`model/fx_table.h:4-8`; ADR 0004: 43–127 reserved for core FX, 128–255 for an engine's own FX). Core FX already defined: 43 Speed, 44 Tempo fine, 45 Order jump (`seq/fx.h:75,168`; ADR 0004 amendment 1). Export to the OG drops core FX and lists them in the drop report.
- The MOD / S3M importer (`docs/formats/mod.md` §4, OS-MODIMP, libopenmpt oracle) maps to MED commands; the **Amiga engine with engine FX** plays them (`audio/med_tick.h`), the **Modern engine** only has the OG / core FX set, so an S3M (Modern by default) loses arpeggio, volume slides, fine slides and slides; both formats drop the effects listed below.

## 2. Rules

- Each FX: an `fx.h` row (kind, group, range), the sequencer / voice implementation on both engines, the picker entry and a value formatter (UI map: FX column, no new UI), oracle tests against libopenmpt's behaviour for the source effect (the importer's oracle harness).
- **Tick semantics**: the engine's slide-on-tick-0 difference (`mod.md` §4) applies; the new FX follow the source trackers (tick 1..) when the import says so. The project-level slide switch stays an open maintainer decision (a format change), not part of this item.
- **Importers** (OS-MODIMP now, OS-XMIT later) map to the new FX on the Modern engine; the report's dropped / approximated counts shrink accordingly.
- Real time: per-tick work bounded per voice; nothing in the SRAM hot slab without measurement; record flash / SRAM / CPU deltas. Protocol and budget (inside the voices' share): `docs/architecture/cpu-budget.md`; not merged until measured on the unit.
- Format: no layout change (reserved ids gain meaning, as ADR 0004 planned); an older OS keeps them inert; OG export drops them and reports.

## 4. CF1 as built (2026-10-14)

- **Ids (ADR 0004 amendment 2):** 46 Arpeggio, 47 Volume slide, 48 Fine volume slide, 49 Fine porta up, 50 Fine porta down; picker indices 43..47 (appended), glyphs `@ ^ ~ < >`, info-line words in `app/fx_text.h`. CF2 and CF3 continue from 51 in the order of the table in section 1.
- **How they run.** They are Row FX: the sequencer sends the voices a `TrkFx` event at the start of the row (type 0 ends the column's effect on the first row that holds none: an effect lasts its row, as in the source trackers, and the importer writes no terminator). `audio/tracker_fx.h` runs them on the Tick events, in flash, through two cold hooks of `VoicePool` (`tracker_view`, `tracker_set`): the pitch goes through the existing Slide offset, the level through `regain`. The Amiga voices take the same events as MED commands (`00`, `0A`, `1A`/`1B`, `11`/`12`).
- **Units.** Arpeggio and slides keep the `xy` nibbles; volume is the tracker's 0..64 (the instrument's `amiga_vol`, or `(velocity + 1) / 2`); a fine slide counts quarter Paula period units (Scream Tracker's: MOD `E1x` is 4 x, S3M `FFx` 4 x, `FEx` x), the channel's period (the note's from the table, scaled by 8363 / the sample's recorded rate as Scream Tracker's C2SPD does it: OS-BUG-MODPLAY F4, `trkfx::note_period_at`; the table's own for a sample with no recorded rate or at 44,100, 8,287 or 8,363 Hz) is held until a new note and the pitch offset is the exact frequency ratio under the voice's linear-between-semitones law (to a tenth of a cent against libopenmpt).
- **Verification.** `corefx_openmpt` (the whole chain against libopenmpt, per tick): 2,618 comparisons, 0 disagreements; `modimp_openmpt` 14,200, 0; unit: `audio_tracker_fx_test.cpp`, `seq_corefx_test.cpp` (including a full event queue at the row that ends an effect), `amiga_voices_test.cpp` (the Amiga-engine mapping of the TrkFx: 00, 0A, 1A / 1B, 11 / 12, `(b + 2) / 4`, the end per column), the importer and table tests. **What the oracle does not cover:** it never sends a zero parameter, so the figures say nothing about parameter memory (below), and they are not a claim of S3M parity, only of agreement on the parameters it sends.
- **S3M parameter memory (resolved in CF2, section 5).** (CF1's text:) Scream Tracker's `D` `E` `F` `J` `K` `L` `Q` `R` `S` with parameter 00 repeat the channel's last parameter; the importer does not resolve it (the row's FX is written with value 00). Seen against libopenmpt: `J37` then `J00` goes flat (libopenmpt keeps the arpeggio 3 7), `D04` then `D00` holds the level (libopenmpt keeps sliding by 4). To be resolved in CF2 (in the importer or in the FX); until then do not rely on a zero parameter in an imported S3M.
- **Unsupported mix: a Slide or Glide together with a tracker FX on one track.** FX 41 / 42 (Slide) or the Glide of OS-IFX1 and a tracker FX (46..50) on the same track are not defined: `tracker_set` writes the pitch offset the Slide ramp also writes, so a tracker FX overwrites the ramp, and a Slide `ModEnd` clears a held fine-porta offset. Not checked: whether an import can write the two on one track; use one or the other on a track.
- **Cost (estimate; measured on the unit with `diag cpu`).** No per-sample path changed, nothing added to SRAM_L but one call in `Engine::dispatch` and one in its FxReset case. A tick pass is a compare per idle track and about 600 cycles per sliding track (one log2 and one exp2 for a pitch change, one exp2 for a level change, a voice lookup): 12 sliding tracks at 125 BPM (50 ticks a second) is about 0.2 % of the CPU; the pitch re-evaluation of the block after a change is the existing Slide path.
- **Open questions.** (1) Scream Tracker's arpeggio plays from the note and forgets a fine slide's period (libopenmpt follows it); ours plays from the slid period, as ProTracker's. (2) A MOD or S3M sample's default volume goes through the instrument law (`Og12`: volume 64 is +24 dB), so a slide on a note that names no velocity moves by that law, not linearly; an imported song that names a velocity (the volume column, the importer's baked volumes) is linear. (3) The pan of MOD `8xx` / `E8x` and S3M `Xxx` / `S8x` is Channel pan (57), written and acted on since the CF2 fold; the Panning FX (31) keeps its OG id. (4) The OG exporter writes 43..127 as none and counts them (`ExportDrops::core_fx`; maintainer decision 2026-10-14, ADR 0004 amendment 1), so an OG export loses the tracker FX; only a native `.o3pj` keeps them. (5) A note delayed by Micro-move starts after its row's tick 0, so a fine slide acts on the voice that was sounding. (6) The slide-on-tick-0 switch is unchanged (maintainer decision).

## 5. CF2 as built (2026-10-14)

- **Ids (ADR 0004 amendment 2):** 51 Pan slide, 52 Vibrato waveform, 53 Tremolo waveform, 54 Glissando, 55 Retrigger, 56 Tremor, 57 Channel pan; picker indices 48..54 (appended), glyphs `= " ' ; / : |`, info-line words in `app/fx_text.h`. CF3 continues from 58. No persistent layout change (reserved ids gain meaning).
- **What each does (Modern engine, `audio/tracker_fx.h`).** *Pan slide* `xy`: the track's pan, absolute on the graph's strip (`Graph::set_track_pan`), counted in 1/256 as libopenmpt does: from tick 1 y right else x left, 4/256 a tick; `xF` fine left x and `Fy` fine right y on tick 0; held after the row. *Waveforms* (`E4x` `S3x` / `E7x` `S4x`): once, kept until the transport starts, the vibrato's (finetune LFO) or tremolo's (amp LFO) shape from the tracker's 0..7 (sine -> triangle, ramp down -> reverse saw, square, random; "no retrigger" is not modelled: the OS LFOs run on the transport). *Glissando* (`E3x` `S1x`): the Glide and Slide ramps of the track are heard in whole semitones (the nearest); a fine slide is not a ramp. *Retrigger* `Qxy`: libopenmpt's count (a restart when the count has reached y, then count the tick; kept by rows without the effect, 0 at a new note), the volume code applied first (`x`: none, -1 -2 -4 -8 -16, x2/3, x1/2, +1 +2 +4 +8 +16, x3/2, x2), then the sample restarts from its start with the old output fading under it. *Tremor* `Ixy`: x + 1 ticks on, y + 1 off, the position counted on every tick of a row that carries it and kept across rows that do not; the gate gives the voice volume 0 and the channel's volume is untouched.
- **Pan: two FX, two lifetimes (CF2 fold, maintainer decision 2026-10-14, review finding).** The *Panning FX (31)* is, in this OS's own model, the instrument pan set to the value, 0..100 identity: every note-on first ends the FX of both columns and then applies the new step's, and the FX is applied at once and again at each note while it is held; it ends at the next note step that does not hold it, on FX Off 01, or when another FX replaces it. Whether the official firmware's Panning FX behaves the same is an open research question (Q). `Engine::dispatch` hands the Panning group's ModSet and ModEnd to the tracker processor (`TrackerFx::takes`, one inline test): the ModSet puts the track's absolute pan (the instrument's offset replaced, 0..100) on the graph's strip at once and again after each note's own pan (`TrackerFx::note_started`, called on the sampler, Amiga and SD module note paths); the sequencer's ModEnd (the next note step without it, FX Off, a replacing FX) gives the instrument's pan back, or the pan the processor holds (57, a pan slide). *Channel pan (57, N)*, new Community OS behaviour, is what a tracker's pan is: `value` 0..100 (50 the centre), the track's absolute pan, set at the row and held until another Channel pan, a pan slide that moves it or the transport start (FxReset), over notes and over the Panning FX's end. A TrkFx of type 57 is consumed like the others of 46..56 (Row kind) but the processor keeps no column state for it (the TrkFx 0 that ends the row ends nothing). Importers: MOD `8xx` (round(xx x 100 / 255)), `E8x` (x x 17), S3M `Xxx` (0..0x80: 2 x min(xx, 0x80), `XA4` surround held to the right) and `S8x` become 57, on every engine (`Cell` command 0x46; 57 only moves the strip, which the Amiga voices do not); the earlier 33 steps through the MED track pan 2E are gone. The OG exporter drops 57 with the rest of 43..127 and counts it in `ExportDrops::core_fx` (no conversion to 31: the lifetimes differ). A pan slide (51) replaces a held Panning FX and goes on from the pan in force; the performance layer's panning is not acted on. Cost in SRAM_L: see the budget line. A MED `2E` track pan, which MED holds, is still imported as the Panning FX (31) and so ends with its step: open (ask the maintainer before changing the OctaMED mapping).
- **Verification.** `corefx_openmpt` (importer -> sequencer -> engine against libopenmpt, per tick): **5,993 comparisons, 0 disagreements** (CF1's 2,618 plus memory with zero parameters: `J37 J00`, `D04 D00`, `D30 D00`, `DF4 D00`, fine E/F shared memory, `Q23 Q00`, `I23 I00`, `P04 P00`; Q with 20 variants of interval and volume code, rows of a run, a gap, a new note; I with 9; pan slide from `S88` in 18, the stereo pan position R / (L + R) against the graph's law). Not compared (no libopenmpt counterpart in the engine's terms): the waveforms and the glissando (unit tests only). Units: `audio_tracker_fx_test.cpp` (the retrigger's count and volume codes, the tremor gate, the pan slide and the Panning FX on a graph, the no-graph case), the waveform override equal, sample for sample, to the same LFO set in the instrument (both LFOs, the eight values, before and after the note, ended by the transport start) and the glissando's whole-semitone blocks, `modimp_import_test.cpp` (the mapping and the memory), the table and picker tests.
- **Cost.** No per-sample path changed. The tick pass is a compare per idle track and, for a track with an effect, a voice lookup and a few integer operations (host bench `tracker FX tick pass` in `o3os_audio_bench`, Release: 12 tracks with an effect each take about 1.2 us a Tick on the host, retrigger, tremor and pan slide within 15 % of CF1's fine porta + volume slide; the table's cycle column scales CF1's estimate of about 600 cycles a pitch-changing track by those ratios, so it is an upper bound: about 7,000 to 8,300 cycles a tick for 12 tracks, 0.2 % of the 180 MHz CPU at 50 ticks a second, 1.2 % at 300). Hot code (SRAM_L, `.ramfunc_l`): `flfo_level` reads the LFO shape from the generator (`g.l_ovr`, one compare), `repitch` is no longer static and tests the track's glissando bit while a ramp runs, and `Engine::dispatch` calls the cold `tracker_event` (the filter `TrackerFx::takes` is in flash) and, after a sampler note's pan, `TrackerFx::note_started`; the budget lines are in the commit messages. CF2 fold (this commit's message): flash +416 B, SRAM_L -24 B (`.ramfunc_l` 27,496 B of 27,648 B), SRAM_U +0, against the previous build; with CF2's own (+2,992 B flash, +80 B SRAM_L) CF2 costs +3,408 B flash and +56 B SRAM_L over CF1. The strip's pan work is `Graph::set_track_pan` / `track_pan`, in flash. Measure on the unit with `diag cpu`.
- **Open questions.** (1) A MED `2E` track pan is imported as the Panning FX (31), which ends with its step, where MED's holds (above). (2) S3M `XA4` (surround) is imported as the right, not as a surround. (3) The waveform's sine is the triangle; the tracker's retrigger flag has nothing to switch. (4) A one-shot sample that ended is gone: a later retrigger has nothing to restart (libopenmpt restarts it). (5) A strip pan edited live while the processor holds the track's pan shifts the offset it computed (the processor's pan is absolute against the strip's value at the time). Review of the fold (2026-10-14, APPROVE WITH MINORS), accepted as limits: (6) S3M `Xxx` above 0x80 other than `A4` is imported as hard right (libopenmpt is believed to ignore it, not checked; the oracle compares up to 0x80). (7) `TrackerFx::reset()` sets the held instrument pan to 50, so a Panning FX (31) applied and ended before any note since play started gives back 50; the next note corrects it. (8) A performance-layer ModSet of the Panning group is not acted on, so a sequencer FX 31 that ends while the perform layer holds the group keeps the pan until that layer ends.

## 6. CF3 as built (2026-10-14)

- **Ids (ADR 0004 amendment 2):** 58 Channel volume, 59 Channel volume slide, 60 Global volume, 61 Global volume slide, 62 Panbrello; picker indices 55..59 (appended after Channel pan), glyphs `[ ] ( ) {`, info-line words in `app/fx_text.h` (`CHVOL 32`, `CHVSL -4` / `CHVSL FINE +3`, `GVOL 48`, `GVSL +4`, `PANBR SPD 3 DEP 10` / `DEP KEEP`). Table ranges: 58 and 60 are 0..64 (default 64), the others any byte. Importer (S3M, Modern engine): Cell commands 0x47..0x4B for `M N V W Y`. No persistent layout change.
- **What each does (`audio/tracker_fx.h`).** *Channel volume* `0..64` and *Global volume* `0..64` are set once and HELD like Channel pan: over the notes and the rows, until another one, a slide that moves it, the transport start (FxReset) or, new in CF3, the transport stop (`Stopped`), so that a song that ends at volume 0 (a fade-out) does not leave the pads silent (a stop followed by a resume therefore resumes at full volume: NEW Community OS behaviour, accepted 2026-10-14, open question 3). The strips' gains do not snap at the stop: the states go to full, but a strip whose gain was below full keeps it (`TrackerFx::gain_stale_`, one bit a track) until that track's next note (`note_started`: a pad, the next song's first note) or the transport start (`FxReset`), so the release tails of the stopped voices keep ringing at the level they had instead of swelling to full when `release_all()` has left them playing. No per-sample work. *Channel volume slide* `Nxy` and *Global volume slide* `Wxy` run on the Tick events of their rows in libopenmpt's forms: from tick 1 one step of 1/64 a tick, the channel's going down first (`y` down else `x` up) and the global one up first (`x` up else `y` down, as libopenmpt's two functions differ), `xF` fine up x and `Fy` fine down y once on tick 0 (an `FF` is +15), held to 0..64 and kept after the row; two tracks that carry `W` on a row slide the global volume twice. *Panbrello* `Yxy` (x speed, y depth, a zero nibble keeps the track's last: libopenmpt's per-nibble memory, kept in the engine, so the importer sends `Y00` as it is): each tick of a row that carries it the pan moves by `(sine64((pos + 16) >> 2) x depth + 2) / 8` of 256 (libopenmpt's S3M panbrello with its 64-point sine table, from a 17-byte quarter table here) and the position advances by the speed; the position is kept over rows AND over notes (libopenmpt's S3M does not restart it at a note: measured, `pb_renote`) and the offset ends on the first row that does not carry the effect (the S3M form: IT holds it, not modelled).
- **Where the gain lives (the maintainer's question: a gain the engine already has a place for).** The volumes are not a new multiply in the voices or the kernels and not the voice's volume (`regain`: that is the note's own law, and the tracker's channel volume multiplies it): they are the strip's gain, `Graph::set_track_gain(track, channel volume x global volume / 4096)`, one more factor in `Graph::recompute_strip`'s fader product (`level = fader x dry x tracker gain`; the sends too, pre- or post-fader: the gain is the note's, like the volume column, and a pre-fader send skips only the fader; tails already in the delay or reverb decay by themselves), applied through the strip's existing per-block `level` smoother (Q16, ramped over the block like every fader move), with no per-sample work added (the strip kernel already multiplies the smoothed level). A global volume change retargets the strips once, at the tick that moves it. The panbrello is `Graph::set_track_pan_mod`, an offset added to `pan + pan_offset` inside `recompute_strip`, so it rides on whatever pan is in force (the strip's, the instrument's, the Panning FX, Channel pan, a pan slide) and changes none of them; the pan law clamps the sum as libopenmpt clamps its pan. Both are in flash (`O3OS_AUDIO_COLD`), called once a tick per changed strip; the Amiga voices are not involved (the strip is engine-independent, so a TrkFx 58..62 acts on an Amiga track's strip too, though the importer writes them only for the Modern engine).
- **Verification.** `corefx_openmpt` (importer -> sequencer -> engine against libopenmpt, per tick): **9,490 comparisons, 0 disagreements** (CF2's 5,993 plus the CF3 cases: M with 5 values and a note over it, M above 64 ignored, N in 15 variants including both nibbles, the fine forms `xF` `Fy` and `FF` `0F` `F0`, memory with `N00`, the product of the note's own slide and the channel volume; V with 5 values including 50 (libopenmpt ignores it above 64, as the importer does) and a note over it, the product of V and M, W in 15 variants including its x-first order, its memory, and N's memory being not W's; Y in 12 variants plus a note over it, compared as the stereo position per tick). One accepted difference in the panbrello's measurement, not in the effect: the graph recomputes the pan once per 64-sample block, so where the panbrello's pan steps at a tick boundary the last up to 43 samples of the previous tick (inside the measured window) are already ramping; the pan cases of the panbrello allow 0.7 of the next tick's step as slack (`next_slack`), the other pan cases are as in CF2. Units: `audio_tracker_fx_test.cpp` (the step functions against libopenmpt's forms, the sine against `round(127 sin)` for all 64 points, the held volumes, the product, the slides, FxReset and Stopped, the panbrello's positions by hand, the nibbles' memory, the pan in force untouched), `audio_graph_test.cpp` (the strip's gain scales the fader, the pan offset moves the law, both held to range), `modimp_import_test.cpp` (the mapping, N and W memory, M41 ignored, the engine window drops them), the table, picker, words, sequencer and OG-export tests (`ExportDrops::core_fx` counts them).
- **Cost.** Budget lines (mk66, `tools/ci.sh mk66`, against the previous build measured in a clean checkout): flash 608,524 B (+2,140 B), SRAM_L 49,272 B (-8 B), SRAM_U 135,432 B (+0), `.ramfunc_l` 27,000 B of 27,648 B (-8 B; `check_ramfunc` passes); tone image flash 488,720 B (+2,128 B), SRAM_L 49,272 B (-8 B), SRAM_U 89,440 B (+0). The tick pass adds, for a track with a CF3 effect, an integer step and, when a value changed, one float multiply and a `set_track_gain` (a compare, a mark); a panbrello adds a table lookup, a multiply and one float multiply a tick; the per-strip work is `recompute_strip` (about a few hundred cycles) once per changed strip per tick: 16 strips on a global volume slide at 50 ticks a second is about 5,000 cycles a tick, 0.14 % of the 180 MHz CPU (an estimate; measure with `diag cpu`, QA-PAT-149). No per-sample path changed and nothing was added to SRAM_L but the strip's two floats (`Strip::pan_mod`, `trk_gain`, 8 bytes a strip, in SRAM_U) and the `+ pan_mod` and the gain product inside the cold `recompute_strip`.
- **Open questions.** (1) IT `Vxx` is 0..128 and IT `Wxy` steps half as far as S3M's on the 64 scale, XM `Gxx` / `Hxy` are the S3M scale; there is no XM or IT importer yet, so Global volume is the 0..64 S3M / XM scale (an IT importer maps `V` / 2 and loses the last bit, or Global volume grows a 0..128 range: maintainer or OS-XMIT's decision). (2) The S3M header's initial global volume is carried as a `V` (FX 60) at row 0 of the first playable pattern (the 254/255 markers skipped) when it is below 64, that row has a free FX slot on some track and the pattern appears once in the order list; otherwise (no free slot, a reused first pattern, the engine window, a value above 64) it stays in the report as dropped (`ImportReport::global_volume_written`). When the song loops back to that pattern (the end of the order list), the `V` is applied again, so a song that changed its global volume restarts at the header's value on the loop (Scream Tracker keeps the changed value). An S3M `M` or `V` above 64 is counted as dropped too. (3) Stop resets the volumes (above): a stop and resume resumes at full volume. (4) `S5x` (the panbrello's waveform) and IT's held panbrello (its table, its sample-and-hold random) are not modelled; the waveform is the sine. (5) A pattern with Y in both FX columns of a track advances the position twice a tick. (6) The sends follow the volumes, pre- or post-fader (decision D3, 2026-10-14: the channel and global volume are the note's gain, as in IT and S3M; only the mixer fader is skipped by a pre-fader send), so a global fade to 0 leaves the bus inputs silent while the tails already in the effects decay naturally. (7) Latent, not fixed: the sequencer's `emit()` result is ignored for the held types 57, 58 and 60, so a full event queue loses the held value for the rest of the song (a later row of the same effect restores it).
