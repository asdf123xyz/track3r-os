# track3r-os

A community operating system for the Polyend Tracker hardware, written from scratch.

!!! warning "Unofficial"
    track3r-os is unofficial community firmware for the Polyend Tracker. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Polyend. Installing third-party firmware is at your own risk; keep the official firmware at hand to go back.
