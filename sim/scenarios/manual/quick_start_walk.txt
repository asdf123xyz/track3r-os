# OS-MANUAL2 C4: the newcomer path of docs/manual/quick_start.md ("New to trackers?"). Each `snap walk_<n>` is the picture of step n, `quick_start_walk_<n>.png` in docs/manual/img/ (manifest docs/manual/images.txt).
# The manual numbers the pads 1 to 48 and the simulator's `pad` takes 0 to 47: the manual's pad N is `pad N-1`. The simulator has no sequencer: `playhead 6` is the word the engine publishes while a pattern plays.
# Step 2: File > NEW > KEEP INSTRUMENTS. The project keeps the demo kit's instruments and has a new, empty pattern (the demo's own pattern 1 holds a two-bar beat).
document new
wait 20
tap file
wait 20
tap f3
wait 20
tap f1
wait 900
assert state screen=PATTERN
wait 3000

# Steps 1 and 2 need no picture: the logo and the demo kit are the boot's, the NEW question is a strip text, and the pad press of WATCH changes nothing on the screen.
# Step 3. Press `Rec`, then four pads. The chip reads STEP, and each pad writes a note on the cursor's step and moves the cursor on.
tap rec
wait 20
pad 0
pad 4
pad 7
pad 11
wait 20
snap walk_3

# Step 4. Press `Play`. The header reads PLAY and a green bar, the playhead, runs down the steps. Press `Play` again to stop.
tap play
playhead 6
wait 100
snap walk_4
tap play
playhead stop
wait 20

# Step 5. Press `File`, then `F4` (SAVE): the strip says SAVE TRACK 01 DONE. No picture and no step here: a save needs the simulator's `card` (a fixture folder that `o3sim --cards` names), and the File screen shows the strip only.
# (Checked by hand once with `card e2e_card`: the strip read SAVE TRACK 01 DONE.)
