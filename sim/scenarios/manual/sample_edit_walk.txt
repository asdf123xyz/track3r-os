# OS-MANUAL2 C3: the pictures of the Sample edit chapter's tasks (docs/manual/sample_edit.md, a brief chapter: no "Try it", the pictures sit in the tasks): select a part, normalise it, take it back.
# The simulator's demo sample draws no waveform, so the selection and the processes show nothing to snap: this scenario checks the keys of the chapter's tasks (the screen stays Sample edit), and the chapter has no walk pictures.

# Select a part. Turn the encoder: a selection appears and the encoder moves its start edge. `Right` picks the end edge; turn the encoder to move it.
document sound
wait 30
tap sampleedit
wait 20
tap right
encoder 20
wait 20
assert state screen=~SAMPLE

# Press `F2` (NORM): the range's peak goes to full scale (or the strip says NOTHING TO NORMALISE).
tap f2
wait 20
assert state screen=~SAMPLE

# Press `F8` (UNDO): the sample is as it was.
tap f8
wait 20
assert state screen=~SAMPLE
