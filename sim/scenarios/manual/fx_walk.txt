# OS-MANUAL2 C2: the walk-through of the FX chapter's "Try it" (docs/manual/fx.md). Each `snap walk_<n>` is the picture of step n, `fx_walk_<n>.png` in docs/manual/img/
# (the manifest is docs/manual/images.txt; tools/manual_images.py renders it, ctest o3sim_manual_images keeps the committed pictures in step). A new step or a changed key means a changed
# picture: edit the chapter's step and this line together.
#
# The effect is Reverse (letter Q), the first effect the list reaches from an empty cell: 14 detents from None. The list's picker order is the order the chapter's table shows.
# The simulator has no sound, so the chapter's step that listens has no picture and no line here.
document
wait 20

# Step 1. Press `Pattern`, then `Rec` (STEP), then `Fx`. The cursor is on the first effect cell, FX1, of step 1 of track 1.
tap pattern
wait 20
tap rec
wait 20
tap fx
wait 20
assert state screen=PATTERN
snap walk_1

# Step 2. Hold `Vol`. The FX list opens with a description under it. Turn the encoder 14 detents: Reverse is highlighted, and its description says what it does.
press vol
wait 700
encoder 14
wait 20
snap walk_2

# Step 3. Let go of `Vol`. The effect is entered: the cell shows its letter, Q.
release vol
wait 50
snap walk_3

# Step 4. Turn the encoder up. The value stops at its last value, 01, and does not wrap.
encoder 5
wait 20
snap walk_4

# Step 5. Press `Fx` again. The cursor moves to the second effect cell, FX2; FX1 keeps Q01.
tap fx
wait 20
snap walk_5
