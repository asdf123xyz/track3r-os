# OS-MANUAL2 C3: the walk-through of the Mixer chapter's "Try it" (docs/manual/mixer.md): route a track to the cue output, look at the master page, mute and unmute, set the master volume.
# Each `snap walk_<n>` is the picture of step n, `mixer_walk_<n>.png` in docs/manual/img/. A changed step is a changed picture: edit both together.

# Step 1. Press `Master`: the MIXER page, a column for each track.
document
wait 30
tap master
wait 20
assert state screen=MIXER
snap walk_1

# Step 2. Press `F2` (ROUTING): a row for each track, a column for each place it can go.
tap f2
wait 20
snap walk_2

# Step 3. On the row of track 1 go right to the CUE column (MAIN, CUE) and turn the encoder up: track 1 now goes to the cue output as well as MAIN.
tap right
encoder 1
wait 20
snap walk_3

# Step 4. Press `F3` (MST FX): the MASTER FX page, the master section, the delay and reverb returns and the controls of the two send effects.
tap f3
wait 20
snap walk_4

# Step 5. Press `Left` (the column is shared with ROUTING: back to track 1), `F1` (MIXER), then `F7` (MUTE) on track 1, then `Shift` + `F7` (UNMUTE) to open every strip again.
tap left
tap f1
wait 20
tap f7
wait 20
snap walk_5
press shift
tap f7
release shift
wait 20

# Step 6. Hold `Master` and turn the encoder: the master volume moves on any screen. The strip says MASTER VOLUME.
tap pattern
wait 20
press master
wait 5
encoder -7
wait 20
assert state screen=PATTERN
snap walk_6
release master
