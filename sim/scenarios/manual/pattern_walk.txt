# OS-MANUAL2 C4: the "Try it" of docs/manual/pattern.md. Each `snap walk_<n>` is the picture of step n, `pattern_walk_<n>.png` in docs/manual/img/ (manifest docs/manual/images.txt).
# The manual numbers the pads 1 to 48 and the simulator's `pad` takes 0 to 47: the manual's pad N is `pad N-1`. Nothing here needs the engine: the steps write notes and move the playhead.

# Setup, not a step: the empty project (File > NEW, ALL), so the walk-through starts on one empty pattern, as a new project does.
document new
wait 20
tap file
wait 20
tap f3
wait 20
tap f3
wait 700
assert state screen=PATTERN
wait 3000

# Step 1. Press `Pattern`. The grid opens in WATCH, the browse mode: pads only preview. No picture: the screen at a glance shows the screen.
tap pattern
wait 700

# Step 2. Press `Rec`. The chip reads STEP and a red frame shows. No picture of its own: the screen at a glance shows the STEP state.
tap rec
wait 20

# Step 3. Press pad 1, pad 5, pad 8 and pad 12. Each note is written on the cursor's step, and the cursor moves on by JUMP.
pad 0
pad 4
pad 7
pad 11
wait 20
snap walk_3

# Step 4. Press `Play`. The playhead runs over the steps (the simulator has no sequencer: `playhead 6` is the word the engine publishes). A STEP take is not moved by it.
tap play
playhead 6
wait 100
snap walk_4

# Step 5. Press `Play` to stop, then `Rec`. The chip reads WATCH, and the four notes stay in the grid.
tap play
playhead stop
wait 20
tap rec
wait 20
snap walk_5
