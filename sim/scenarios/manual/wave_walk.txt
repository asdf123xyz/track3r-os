# OS-MANUAL2 C3: the walk-through of the Wave chapter's "Try it" (docs/manual/wave.md): open the play modes, choose WAVETABLE, halve the window, choose SLICE, cut the sample in even parts.
# The simulator's demo instrument is a sine with no sample, so the WAVETABLE and CHOP views show "no sample" here: the chapter's pictures are the gallery states (wave_qa_wav_003, wave_qa_wav_004). This scenario checks the keys of the steps.

# Step 1. Press `Sample Play`: the WAVE page of the instrument in view.
document sound
wait 30
tap sampleplay
wait 20
assert state screen=WAVE

# Step 2. Press `Shift` + `F1` (MODE): the list of the eight play modes; NOW marks the instrument's own.
press shift
tap f1
release shift
wait 20
assert state screen=WAVE

# Step 3. Press `Down` six times to WAVETABLE and `F1` (SELECT): the WAVETABLE view, a window marked on the sample.
tap down 6
tap f1
wait 20
assert state screen=WAVE

# Step 4. Press `F2` (WINDOW) and turn the encoder down one click: the window halves and the same part of the sample stays marked.
tap f2
encoder -1
wait 20
assert state screen=WAVE

# Step 5. Press `Shift` + `F1`, `Up` twice to SLICE, `F1`: the CHOP page, with no slices yet.
press shift
tap f1
release shift
tap up 2
tap f1
wait 20
assert state screen=WAVE

# Step 6. Press `F2` (EVEN): the sample is cut in equal parts, drawn as lines.
tap f2
wait 20
assert state screen=WAVE
