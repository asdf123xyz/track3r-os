# OS-MANUAL2 C4: the "Try it" of docs/manual/pattern_tools.md. Each `snap walk_<n>` is the picture of step n, `pattern_tools_walk_<n>.png` in docs/manual/img/ (manifest docs/manual/images.txt).
# The pad and F-key presses are the chapter's own.

# Setup, not a step: the empty project (File > NEW, ALL), so the walk-through starts on one empty pattern, as a new project does.
document new
wait 20
tap file
wait 20
tap f3
wait 20
tap f3
wait 700
assert state screen=PATTERN
wait 3000

# Step 1. Press `Pattern`, then `Rec`. The chip reads STEP. No picture: the Pattern chapter shows it.
wait 700
tap rec
wait 20
assert state screen=PATTERN

# Step 2. Press `F4` (FILL). The Fill dialog opens on TRACK / NOTE / EVERY N.
tap f4
wait 20
snap walk_2

# Step 3. `Down` twice and `Right` twice: TYPE reads EUCLID. `Down` and `Right` once more: EVENTS 5 (from 4). The line shows the five hits.
tap down 2
tap right 2
tap down
tap right
wait 20
snap walk_3

# Step 4. Press `F1` (FILL). The notes land on the five hits, the dialog closes, the strip says FILL: EUCLID 5 EVENTS.
tap f1
wait 20
snap walk_4

# Step 5. Press `F7` (TOOLS). The list opens with EXPORT PATTERN first, then DUPLICATE x2, EXPAND x2 and SHRINK /2. No picture: step 6 shows the list's result.
wait 3000
tap f7
wait 20

# Step 6. `Down` twice, to EXPAND x2, and `F1` (RUN). Each note moves to every second step and the strip names the new length.
tap down 2
tap f1
wait 20
snap walk_6

# Step 7. Press `F8` (UNDO). The length comes back. No picture.
tap f8
wait 20
