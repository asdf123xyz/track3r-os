# OS-CENTER and OS-PATCENTER (new Community OS behaviour; maintainer, 2026-10-17): the cursor's row is on the centre slot (the 8th: seven rows above it)
# at EVERY step, the first and the last included: at step 1 the seven slots above it are blank, at the last step the eight below it. A document: 64 steps, the window shows 16.
document
snap start

# The first row is on the centre slot with seven blank slots above it (snap start); the blank slots fill with steps as the cursor goes down (at row 7 the window starts at step 01).
action nav.scroll 7
snap row_7

# One more and the pattern scrolls on: the cursor stays on the centre slot, row 8 shows step 02 first.
tap down
snap row_8

action nav.scroll 20
snap row_28

# The last row is on the centre slot too, with eight blank slots below it.
action cursor.jump_end
snap last_row
action nav.scroll -3
snap row_60

# A jump to the middle centres it; back at the top the cursor is on the centre slot again, the slots above it blank.
action cursor.jump_fraction 2
snap jump_middle
action cursor.jump_fraction 0
snap top_again
