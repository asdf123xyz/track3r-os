# OS-E2E-9: the instrument filter against a textbook reference (tools/dsp_ref.py with scipy, tests/e2e_dsp_ref.py filter, ctest e2e_ref_dsp_filter). The deep user's way, as e2e_audio_params: the
# Instrument page (PARAMS), the FILTER page (F3), the encoder, a pad. Instrument 2 of `document sound` is white noise (0.5 of full scale, looped), so the take of a filtered note divided by the
# take of the open one, spectrum against spectrum, IS the filter's magnitude response. Five takes of 300 ms (the pad is down for 250): the noise open; the low-pass at cutoff 0.45 and at 0.65;
# the same with the resonance up to 3.0 (Q 3.7: a peak at the cutoff); the high-pass at 0.65 with the resonance back at 0. The cutoff c is a fraction of the stored range: the engine's table
# makes it int(c x 477) steps of 20 Hz x 2^(step / 50), so 0.45 is 388.6 Hz and 0.65 is 1.47 kHz (audio/svf.h). The test holds each take to the analogue second-order state variable filter at that
# cutoff and Q within its tolerances (written in the test: 0.75 dB up to 6.4 kHz, 3 dB above, where a Chamberlin filter's half-step mean is a little lower than the prototype).
document sound
audio on
wait 30
tap params
tap f7
assert state screen=INSTRUMENT

render open 300
pad 12 hold 250
wait 49

# FILTER page, FILTER on, the cursor on CUTOFF (FILTER, TYPE, CUTOFF, RESONANCE): 1.0 down to 0.45, then up to 0.65.
tap f3
encoder 1
assert model inst.2.filter=1
tap down 2
encoder -55
assert model inst.2.cutoff=0.45
render lp45 300
pad 12 hold 250
wait 49
encoder 20
assert model inst.2.cutoff=0.65
render lp65 300
pad 12 hold 250
wait 49

# The resonance row: 70 steps of 0.043 is 3.01, Q = 3.7.
tap down
encoder 70
assert model inst.2.resonance=3.01
render lp65q 300
pad 12 hold 250
wait 49

# Back to no resonance, and the TYPE row up one: 1 is the high-pass.
encoder -70
assert model inst.2.resonance=0
tap up 2
encoder 1
assert model inst.2.filter_type=1
render hp65 300
pad 12 hold 250
wait 49
assert audio open sound
assert audio lp45 sound
assert audio hp65 sound
