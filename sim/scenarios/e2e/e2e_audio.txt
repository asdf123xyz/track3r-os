# OS-E2E-2, the first journey with sound (docs/specs/OS-E2E.md "E2E-2: audio in the sim"). `audio on` puts the real engine behind the app: the sequencer, the sampler voices, the graph, the send effects and the
# master limiter, rendered by the simulator's own clock (sim_audio.h). `render <name> <ms>` captures the master for the next <ms> of simulated time, and `assert audio` measures it. The document of the audio
# journeys is `document sound`: an empty pattern at 120 BPM and four neutral instruments (volume 50 = 0 dB, pan centre, no filter, no envelope) bound to the test bank: instrument 1 is a sine of 100 samples a period
# (441.18 Hz at the engine's rate, 0.5 of full scale) that loops. No file is read and no WAV is kept in git. `o3sim --wav <dir>` writes every render for listening (build-os-host/sim/wav/ in ctest).
#
# Tolerances, and why: a pitch is within 1-2 cents (the measure is good to a cent, tests/audio_check_test.cpp); a level within 0.1 dB (the sample is integer, the gain unity); the onset within 1.5 ms (a pad goes
# to the engine at the next block start and the voice begins in that block: a block is 1.45 ms, and the pacing of the simulated clock puts the block edges the same every run).
document sound
audio on
wait 30
assert state screen=PATTERN playing=0

# A pad is a live note. Pad 12 is the root of the pad board, note 60 (C-5), which plays a sample at its own pitch. Held 300 ms in a window of 600.
render note 600
pad 12 hold 300
wait 299

# The right note, at the right level, clean: the steady part of the note (a window of 280 ms is 123 periods, so the DC of an open half cycle is under 0.1 % of full scale).
assert audio note in=20-300 pitch=441.18+-2c rms=-9.03+-0.1 peak=-6.02+-0.1 dc<0.002 clicks=0 pan=C+-0.1
# At the right time: the pad went down in the first tick of the window, the engine takes it at the next block start (the note's first sample comes 1.5 ms in).
assert audio note onset=1.5+-1.5
# The whole note, its attack ramp (32 samples) and its release (256) included, has no click: the engine's ramps are kinks, not steps.
assert audio note clicks=0 sound
# It stops: the pad went up at 301 ms, the release is 5.8 ms; the rest of the window is exact silence.
assert audio note in=320-600 silent
assert audio note in=0-300 sound

# Other pads, other notes: an octave down (pad 24, note 48), a whole tone up (pad 14, note 62), a fifth up (pad 19, note 67), an octave up (pad 0, note 72). An octave is a step of 0.5 or 2 samples
# and reads the sample as it is (the alias figure is the 16-bit rounding); the others go through the voices' linear interpolation, which puts the images of the tone about 76 dB under it.
render down 350
pad 24 hold 250
wait 99
render tone 350
pad 14 hold 250
wait 99
render fifth 350
pad 19 hold 250
wait 99
render up 350
pad 0 hold 250
wait 99
assert audio down in=20-250 pitch=220.59+-1c alias<-95 clicks=0 peak=-6.02+-0.1
assert audio tone in=20-250 pitch=495.20+-1c alias<-70 clicks=0 peak=-6.02+-0.1
assert audio fifth in=20-250 pitch=661.02+-1c alias<-70 clicks=0 peak=-6.02+-0.1
assert audio up in=20-250 pitch=882.35+-1c alias<-90 clicks=0 peak=-6.02+-0.1
# The same notes against each other: an octave is a ratio of two, to within a cent.
assert audio up in=20-250 pitch=@note*2+-1c
assert audio down in=20-250 pitch=@note*0.5+-1c
