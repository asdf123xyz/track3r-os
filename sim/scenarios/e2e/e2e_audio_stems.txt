# OS-E2E-2: the stems add up to the master. Two pads down at once: the live notes take two tracks (T1 and T2) and the stem tap, which is what the USB stems carry, copies each track's post-fader block. With
# every strip at the centre, no sends and the master at unity, the master is the sum of the tracks (the saturating sum of the stem tap) 63 frames later: the master limiter looks 63 samples ahead
# (MasterLimiter::kLatency) and is transparent while the sum stays under its ceiling. Instrument 1 is at volume 40 so that two sines of 0.5 sum to under 0.97 and the limiter does nothing.
document sound
audio on
wait 30
tap params
tap f2
encoder -10
assert model inst.1.volume=40
render mix 800 stems
pad 12 down
pad 14 down
wait 400
pad 12 up
pad 14 up
wait 396
# Each pad is its own note on its own track: C-5 (441.18 Hz) on T1, D-5 (a whole tone up, 495.20 Hz) on T2; nothing on T3.
assert audio mix sound
assert audio mix.t1 in=20-400 sound pitch=441.18+-2c rms=-13.83+-0.3
assert audio mix.t2 in=20-400 sound pitch=495.20+-2c rms=-13.83+-0.3
assert audio mix.t3 silent
# The master is the two of them, summed, 63 frames on; the residual is under the 16-bit floor (a null at -200 is exact).
assert audio mix null=mix.t1+mix.t2,-90,lag=63
# The master pair of the stem tap is the master the codec gets, sample for sample.
assert audio mix.m null=mix,-120
# OS-E2E-4: the audio fingerprint (tests/audio_fingerprints/e2e_audio_stems.json): the master and the two tracks that sound.
fingerprint mix nof0
fingerprint mix.t1
fingerprint mix.t2
