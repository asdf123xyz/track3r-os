# OS-E2E-9: a Scream Tracker 3 module played in the simulator and compared with libopenmpt (tests/e2e_openmpt_ref.py, ctest e2e_ref_openmpt_s3m). The same session as e2e_ref_mod: put a card
# in, File > IMPORT, F1 on the module, Play. An S3M is imported for the Modern engine (FX AUTO: its commands become the core tracker FX the sampler voices run), so no engine step is needed.
# The card e2e_ref_s3m is GENERATED (tools/e2e_ref_cards.py: 32 frames of sine at C2SPD 11025, 344.5 Hz at C-4, six cases of two rows at speed 6 and 125 BPM: a tick of 20 ms). The render `song`
# is what the test compares with libopenmpt, tick by tick: the pitch to 1.5 cents and the level to 3 % plus 2 % of the note's.
document
wait 20
card e2e_ref_s3m
audio on
wait 20
tap file
wait 50
tap f6
wait 100
assert state screen=FILE sel=ref.s3m
tap f1
wait 800

assert model tempo=12500
assert model order.len=1
assert model pat.1.0.0.note=61
assert model inst.1.name=sine

# Play from the first row: 12 rows of 120 ms, 1,440 ms.
render song 1480
